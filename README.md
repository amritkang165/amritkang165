&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**things i built that kept me up past 3am for the right reasons —**

<br>

**[🏙️ MERAWARD](https://github.com/amritkang165/meraward)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;→ civic accountability PWA for Delhi's 250 wards. stand anywhere, find your ward, report by voice or photo, get a formal complaint letter in Hindi and English. see which wards the system keeps ignoring. built at First Commit · Bharat Builds Tour 2026 (WeMakeDevs × AWS). we were team Sleepy Peeps. i led the team.
&nbsp;&nbsp;`Lambda` `Bedrock` `DynamoDB` `React 18` `MapLibre` `Python`

<br>

**[🧠 iffy.ai](https://github.com/amritkang165/iffy-ai)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;→ what if you could map the consequences of a belief before you adopted it. iffy builds a typed consequence graph instead of prose — structured, traceable, repairable. top 7 of 350+ teams at ProdX × NAVERA 26.
&nbsp;&nbsp;`Groq` `Zod` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL`

<br>

**[🍩 Pocki](https://github.com/amritkang165/Pocki)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;→ screenshot any UPI app. Pocki reads it. zero manual entry, Vision framework OCR, analytics that actually make sense of where your money went. native iOS, built for how people actually spend in India.
&nbsp;&nbsp;`SwiftUI` `SwiftData` `Vision`

<br>

**[🤖 exHacker](https://github.com/amritkang165/exHacker)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;→ challenge statement in. production-ready blueprint out. under 30 seconds. i built this because teams waste the first 3 hours of every hackathon arguing about stack choices. now they don't have to.
&nbsp;&nbsp;`LangGraph` `LangChain` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `Docker`

<br>

**[🧬 ReGit](https://github.com/amritkang165/ReGit)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;→ git for research. content-addressed artifacts, semantic diffing, three-way prose merge, CRDT concurrency. because `git diff` on a PDF is a crime and the research community deserves better version control.
&nbsp;&nbsp;`Python` `CRDT` `content-addressed storage`

<br>

**[👕 WearWise](https://github.com/amritkang165/wearwise)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; AI wardrobe manager. track what you own, stop wearing the same 3 things. `Next.js` `Prisma` `Gemini`

**[📱 Gitty](https://github.com/amritkang165/gitty)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; mobile GitHub client. because PRs at 2am in bed should be comfortable. `React Native` `TypeScript`

**[🎨 TintAudit](https://github.com/amritkang165/TintAudit)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; WCAG contrast tool. built in Swift compiled to WebAssembly via Tokamak. yes, really. `Swift` `WASM`

**[🐹 Gotermi](https://github.com/amritkang165/Gotermi)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; a terminal pet in Go that watches your coding habits and reacts. `Go` `CLI`

**[💸 x402-in](https://github.com/amritkang165/x402-in)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; agent-to-agent commerce. AI proposes, rules decide, humans authorize money. Razorpay settlement. `Python`

**[🌿 cherrypick](https://github.com/amritkang165/cherrypick)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; accountability app for women builders. deterministic core, FastAPI. `Python`
